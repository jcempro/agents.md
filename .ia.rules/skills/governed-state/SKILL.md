# Governed state

Read `.ia.rules/state/index.json` first. Load only the referenced active FT, the applicable TODO root, and any directly cited decision or evidence.

1. Preserve the immutable TODO governance header and the perennial unchecked Equalizer.
2. Distinguish request, FT registration, normative authorization, code authorization, implementation, and human validation.
3. Resume the canonical FT from its next action; do not replay completed work without a material change.
4. Before code, verify that the normative FT is concluded and that explicit human authorization exists.
5. Keep `continue.ia` resumable; route durable observations to `memory.md`, corrections to `fix.md`, and completed FT pointers to `FT.implementados.md`.
6. Leave implemented operational TODOs at `✅` until human approval authorizes their complete removal.

Use deterministic project scripts for parsing, hashes, migration, or rendering. Do not treat memory, external tools, or this Skill as authority.
